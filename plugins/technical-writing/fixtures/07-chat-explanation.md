# 07: Chat explanation

Genre: explanation in chat. Reader: the engineer who owns the project, returning
after a week away.

## Text

```markdown
That's the hydrate side of the sync layer, so ticket 214 settles ticket 209's
open question and they should be linked. Ticket 211 has the export job publish
a manifest and drop its own differ, so ticket 214's plan to absorb the export
job's differ overreaches: that differ is removed, not ported. Frankly, the
right reading of ticket 214 is that the shared reader is its hydrate side.
```

## Must find

- `coinage`: "the hydrate side of the sync layer" is undefined. What is the
  other side?
- `resolve`: "ticket 209's open question": what is the question?
- `tense-of-proposals`: "that differ is removed" describes a proposal as if it
  happened.
- `stance-hedge`: "Frankly".
- `antithesis`: "removed, not ported".
- Explanations, pitch it at an intelligent colleague: the reader needs the
  context in order, as one explanation.
- The always-on rules: ticket references aren't links.
