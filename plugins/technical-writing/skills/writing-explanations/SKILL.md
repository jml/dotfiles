# Writing explanations

## Procedure

1. **Name the reader and their purpose.** If the requester didn't say who it is
   for, or what the reader will do with it, ask, or state your assumption to the
   requester before drafting.
2. **Load [explanations.md](../../references/genres/explanations.md)** and
   [principles.md](../../references/principles.md).
3. **Find the reader's vocabulary**: the terms in the doc, ticket, or
   conversation they are working from. Use their terms, or map yours onto
   theirs.
4. **Check before you assert.** Separate what you verified from what you
   inferred, and say what you didn't check.
5. **Draft**, leading with what the reader needs first: when they meet the thing,
   for an explanation; the state of play, for a status update; the ask, for a
   request. Use a timeline, table, diagram, or journey where the prose would
   otherwise carry several interacting parts.
6. **Review in a separate pass** wherever principle 9 says to, using the
   `reviewing-prose` skill in its agent-draft posture.
7. **Before sending anything** under someone else's name, show it to them,
   unless they have asked you to send it directly. Text they haven't reviewed
   says a model wrote it.

Every chat reply follows [always-on.md](../../references/always-on.md). This
skill is for the longer pieces.
